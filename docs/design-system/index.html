Sohoj Scan-এর design system। উপরের বোতাম দিয়ে theme বদলে দেখুন। পুরো নিয়ম পড়ুন README.md-তে; Flutter-এ token আসে lib/theme/sohoj_tokens.dart থেকে।

Colour

bg#0B0C0A · #F4F2EB · #000000App background behind every screen. Dark is the default theme.
surface#161814 · #FFFFFF · #000000Cards, sheets, the result panel.
surface-raised#22251F · #E9E6DC · #141414Chips, icon buttons, pressed rows.
ink#F4F2EA · #0E0F0C · #FFFFFFAll primary text and the phone number. 13:1 or more on bg, surface and surface-raised in every theme.
ink-muted#BDBBB1 · #43423D · #E8E8E8Secondary text (time in history, helper lines). Still 8:1 or more on every surface; never lighter.
line#2E312A · #D6D2C6 · #8A8A8ADecorative dividers only. Never the only edge of a control.
line-strong#80837A · #6E6B62 · #FFFFFFBorders of controls (secondary button, cards you tap). 3:1 or more on bg.
signal#C6F432 · #C6F432 · #FFE500The brand lime. Fill for 'number locked' and success: status pill, copied toast, bubble ring. Text on it is on-signal. On light it is a fill only, never text.
on-signal#0B0C0A · #0B0C0A · #000000Text and icons on a signal fill (15:1).
signal-ink#C6F432 · #3B5000 · #FFE500Lime used as text or icon on bg/surface: the check mark, 'locked' label, focus ring. 7:1 or more.
accent#C6F432 · #0E0F0C · #FFE500Primary button fill. Lime on dark, ink on light (lime on a light page has no edge).
on-accent#0B0C0A · #C6F432 · #000000Label and icon on the primary button (15:1).
scan#FFB23F · #FFB23F · #FFFFFFAmber fill for 'searching' state: status pill and scan-frame corners while nothing is locked. Text on it is on-signal.
scan-ink#FFB23F · #6A3C00 · #FFFFFFAmber as text/icon on bg (searching hints).
danger#FF9A8D · #8E2316 · #FF8A80Errors (camera blocked, permission missing). Always with an icon and words.
scrimrgba(0,0,0,0.62) · rgba(0,0,0,0.62) · rgba(0,0,0,0.8)Darkens the camera image outside the scan box so the box reads first.
bubble#0B0C0A · #0B0C0A · #000000Floating bubble body. Always dark so it stands out over any app.

Components

Button

The 88px pill that does the screen's one job. Primary (accent fill) once per screen: on the result screen it is always "কপি করুন". Secondary (2px line-strong border) for the way back: "আবার স্ক্যান". Quiet (surface-raised) for helpers like "শুনুন".

Guidelines: components/Button/README.md

IconButton

A 64px round button for the two controls that live on the camera: flashlight and close. It is the only place an icon goes without a word, so every one carries a TalkBack label ("ফ্ল্যাশলাইট চালু").

Guidelines: components/IconButton/README.md

NumberDisplay

The phone number, always split 3-4-4 (017 1234 5678) in Atkinson Hyperlegible Mono, so 1/7, 0/8 and 5/6 never look alike and the eye can check it in three bites.

01712345678
01898765432
01955550102

Guidelines: components/NumberDisplay/README.md

StatusPill

One pill at the top of the camera that says what the scanner is doing. State is told four ways at once: colour, shape inside (dot / check / alert), the word, and a sound or haptic.

খুঁজছি…পেয়েছিক্যামেরা বন্ধ

Guidelines: components/StatusPill/README.md

OperatorChip

Names the operator from the prefix, under the number. Helps for flexiload, where the operator must be picked.

GrameenphoneRobiAirtelBanglalinkTeletalk

Guidelines: components/OperatorChip/README.md

ScanFrame

The box the number must sit in. Only text inside it is read, which keeps stray numbers on the paper out.

Guidelines: components/ScanFrame/README.md

NumberCard

Used when the paper or screen holds two or more valid numbers. One card per number, the whole card is the tap target.
